## 📖 About

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of the **IEEE Solid-State Circuits Society (SSCS) Student Chapter** at **Universidad Distrital Francisco José de Caldas**.

This organization serves as the technical repository of our chapter, where we preserve and share academic knowledge generated through student projects, research initiatives, technical workshops, competitions, and educational resources.

Our goal is to ensure that every contribution remains accessible for future members, promoting collaboration, reproducibility, and continuous learning.

---

## 🎯 Mission

To build a sustainable technical repository that documents the academic and research activities of the chapter while encouraging innovation, collaboration, and knowledge sharing in the field of integrated circuits and electronic systems.

---

## 📂 Repository Categories

This organization hosts repositories related to:

- 🧪 Research projects
- 🚀 Student engineering projects
- 🏆 IEEE competitions
- 💻 Embedded systems
- ⚡ Analog and Digital IC Design
- 🖥 FPGA developments
- 📚 Educational resources
- 🎓 Technical workshops
- 📄 Publications and documentation

---

## 🌱 Repository Structure

Repositories are organized according to their purpose.

Example:

```
projects/
├── circuit-contest-2026
├── air-quality-monitor
├── fpga-bowling-scoreboard

workshops/
├── workshop-ltspice
├── workshop-kicad

resources/
├── templates
├── references
```

Each repository includes documentation, design files, simulations, reports, and supporting material whenever available.

## 🤝 Contributing

Members of the IEEE SSCS Student Chapter are encouraged to contribute by:

- Documenting their projects.
- Publishing simulation files.
- Uploading reports and presentations.
- Sharing educational resources.
- Improving existing documentation.

Every contribution helps preserve the chapter's technical legacy.
